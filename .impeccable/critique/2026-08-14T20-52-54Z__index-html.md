## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment (A):** Does NOT read as AI-generated overall — but three blocks do. The expensive parts are unmistakably hand-built: the real OSRM trace of R-004 on CARTO tiles with eleven correctly-coloured stops, a genuinely scannable QR, a real editor screenshot, an *offline* BUS-04 at "vu il y a 2 h", a `v0.1.0` build stamp. The gesture layer is a real spring integrator with velocity handoff, rubber-banding, catch-mid-flight, a `dt` floor against `performance.now()` coarsening, and a page clamp. Nothing generates that by accident.

The connective tissue defaulted: `.steps` (three-up chip+heading+paragraph), `.feature-list` (four icons in tinted rounded squares beside a device), and `.trust-grid` — a 2×2 icon-card grid, which is *literally* the pattern PRODUCT.md names in its own anti-references, in the section where a template is most expensive. ## What's Working

- **The gesture layer is the real thing, not a carousel library.** The track is grabbed at its live presentation value rather than its target, so catching it mid-flight never jumps a frame. Release velocity is sampled over an 80ms window with a floor on `dt` because browsers coarsen `performance.now()`. The projected stop is clamped to `startIndex ± max(1, round(dragged))`, so a hard flick advances exactly one screen. Overshoot damping drops to 0.8 *only* when a gesture carried momentum.
- **"Product as imagery" is executed literally, not claimed.** Real route geometry, real stops, correct OSM+CARTO attribution, a scannable QR, an honest offline vehicle, "Données de démonstration" stamped on the map. A director can tell the software exists — which is the conversion argument.
- **Contrast is genuinely strong**, measured from painted pixels rather than computed styles: `.step p` 6.60, dark-section body 7.85, `.lead` on ink 9.23, h2 on ink 17.36, and a correctly-darkened amber at 6.18. Only one pair failed.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Chrome is a light material sitting directly on dark content across the whole CTA panel.** `darkZones` listed only `.sec-ink, .film`; `.cta-panel` — the dark teal surface holding the demo form — was excluded, so a 0.72-alpha near-white bar sat on it and the panel's white lead text ghosted up into the nav row. On the last surface before the page's only action. *Fix: add `.cta-panel` to the probe.*
- **[P1] Body copy has no measure cap — 103 characters per line at 768px.** `.step p` 103cpl, `.tile p` 95–98, `.feature-item p` 87, because the grids collapse to one full-width column and nothing constrains the paragraph. That band is exactly where the target reader sits. *Fix: `max-width: 62ch`.*
- **[P1] With JS off, the page's single conversion goal was unreachable.** Chips are inert `type="button"`s, steps 2–4 carried `inert`, and `#df-send` was `hidden` — no submit control existed at all. The `mailto:` fallback lived only inside a JS `catch`, and the footer carried no email, phone, or WhatsApp. *Fix: stack the steps and let the form POST natively; add a footer contact.*
- **[P2] The header material is too thin.** At 0.72 alpha / blur 20px, the 84px hero headline read straight through the bar and collided with the nav links — reading as a rendering bug rather than as a material. The rule was already written in the CSS comment; only the value fell short.
- **[P2] The sécurité section is a generic icon-card grid at the page's highest-stakes moment**, with zero product proof and no privacy-policy or data-residency statement. It is the one section that shows no software, at the moment a director decides whether to trust you with children's live positions.
- **[P3] The closing band is stock footage of an American yellow school bus** (1.55 MB, credited Pexels) — the final image of a page whose every other visual is Tétouan and Agdal. Peak-end, and an explicit anti-reference violation.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (first-timer):** taps "Notifications" in the phone tab bar — it has an icon, a label, and an active state that visibly moves — and nothing happens; it was a `<div>` with no listener. On form step 3 he waits for the auto-advance he just learned on steps 1–2; the only signal is 13px muted "Plusieurs choix possibles". Arriving mid-page from a WhatsApp link he has **no navigation at all** below 900px.

**Casey (mobile):** 636 KB un-lazy `map-r004.png` above the fold. Scrolling with a thumb over the phone mock, the vertical-bail is correct but she **never discovers the carousel** — the only cues were three dots and a 13px hint.

**Riley (stress tester):** turns JS off → the form dies with no submit button and no email anywhere; **conversion zero**. Flicks the carousel hard and repeatedly → it holds; one screen per flick, catch-and-reverse, rubber-band both ends, resize mid-drag re-snaps. Forces a Formspree failure → gets the prefilled mailto, but it points at **`contact@transtra.app`** while the site's own CNAME is **`transtra.net`**.

**Moroccan school director (real audience):** the sample school is **"Académie Greenwood"** — an English name in a French pitch for the Moroccan market, beside Yasmine Alami and Bab El Had. The console lists **Rabat** neighbourhoods (Agdal, Hay Riad) while the map beside them is **Tétouan**; a local sees it instantly. "Le téléphone du conducteur devient le GPS du bus" raises the obvious question — whose phone, whose data plan, what if it dies mid-route — and the page never answers it.

## Minor Observations

- One genuine AA failure: `p.micro.mock-caption` at **3.54–3.90** (needs 4.5). It sits in the falloff of `.hero-mock`'s 96px shadow, which no ancestor declares as a background — invisible to a DOM-walking contrast checker, caught only by reading painted pixels.
- 61 of 68 `<svg>` lacked `aria-hidden`; every affected control had its own text label, so nothing lost its accessible name — the gap was decorative icons exposed to AT.
- `.pcar-hint` carried `hidden` but still rendered, because its CSS `display` overrode the attribute.
- Step-1/2 chips carried no `aria-pressed`; step-3 chips did.
- `.sec-head { max-width: 40ch }` computes `ch` against *body* size and resolved to 436px at every viewport, breaking a 50px h2 into ~18-character lines.
- All six sections used identical `--sec-pad`, so nothing signalled which section mattered.
